# Slide Storytelling

Slides should support your message, not compete with it.

## Story Arc for Presentations

- Setup: define context and stakes
- Tension: show the challenge or gap
- Resolution: present findings and recommendation

## Slide Design Principles

- One idea per slide
- High-contrast visuals and short text
- Consistent typographic hierarchy
- Intentional pacing with transitions

## Speaker Alignment

1. Use slides as cues, not scripts.
2. Place detail in spoken delivery or handouts.
3. End with a clear action or takeaway.
