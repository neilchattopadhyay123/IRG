# Revision Strategy

Revision is where clarity, structure, and persuasion improve most.

## Three-Pass Model

1. Structural pass: sequence and section logic
2. Argument pass: evidence quality and claim alignment
3. Sentence pass: style, concision, and correctness

## Revision Questions

- Does each section have a clear purpose?
- Are transitions guiding the reader?
- Is evidence interpreted, not just inserted?
- Are there repeated words or filler phrases?

## Fast Improvement Tactics

- Read aloud to catch rhythm and ambiguity.
- Convert passive constructions where clarity suffers.
- Replace abstract language with concrete terms.
- Cut one sentence from every paragraph if possible.
