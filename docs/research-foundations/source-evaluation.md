# Source Evaluation

Evaluating sources protects your argument quality and credibility.

## CRAAP-Inspired Criteria

- Currency: Is the source recent enough for your topic?
- Relevance: Does it directly support your claim?
- Authority: Is the author or publisher credible?
- Accuracy: Are methods and references transparent?
- Purpose: Is there bias, sponsorship, or agenda?

## Cross-Check Routine

1. Verify author expertise.
2. Confirm publication context.
3. Compare with at least two additional reliable sources.
4. Save citation details as you read.

## Red Flags

- No author identity
- No references or weak evidence
- Sensational language without data
- Claims that cannot be independently verified
