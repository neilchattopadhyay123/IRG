# Audience Analysis

Audience analysis helps you shape content before drafting.

## Key Questions

- Who will read or hear this?
- What background knowledge do they already have?
- What concerns, values, or goals matter most to them?
- What action should they take after engaging with your work?

## Practical Method

1. Define primary and secondary audiences.
2. List what each audience knows, believes, and needs.
3. Adjust tone, examples, and detail depth for each audience.
4. Validate your assumptions with feedback.
